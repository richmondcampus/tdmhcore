How the grade works

One number decides it: claims returned this month ÷ claims filed the month before. The previous month is the denominator because a claim returned in June was filed in May — PhilHealth takes weeks to send them back, so dividing by the same month compares June's returns against work that hasn't been reviewed yet.

≤5% = A ≤10% = B ≤15% = C ≤25% = D above 25% = F

Value returned is optional and is never estimated. It appears only for months where the figure was taken from the PhilHealth return listing, and stays blank otherwise — a returned claim is a delay in collection, not a loss, and its amount cannot be worked out from a count.
